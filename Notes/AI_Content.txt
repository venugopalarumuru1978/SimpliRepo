During the course, students will develop:

Knowledge of concepts, techniques, and applications used in AI.
An understanding of AI's impact on daily life.
Knowledge of laws and regulations concerning AI.
The ability to reflect on legal, ethical, economic, and security aspects related to AI.
Knowledge of the similarities and differences between human intelligence and artificial intelligence.

Course Content
AI: Definition, history, and social impact.
Problem-solving with AI.
Application areas.
Search and algorithms.
Prediction and regression analysis.
Machine learning and the importance of data quality.
Classification, object recognition, and learning.
Generative AI.
Artificial General Intelligence (AGI).
